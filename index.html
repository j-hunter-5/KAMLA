FIELD MANUAL v3.2

Compiled from forum posts, field notes, folklore texts and people who learned the hard way.

RULE 01: DOCUMENT FIRST.
Photograph the room before touching anything. Write down the time. Record what changed, not what you think caused it.
RULE 02: HAVE AN EXIT.
If you enter a basement, attic, tunnel, abandoned building or suspiciously cheap motel room, know how you are leaving before you start investigating.
RULE 03: DO NOT WORK ALONE.
Leave your route with somebody. Set a check-in time. If you miss it, they call you first and emergency services second.
RULE 04: COMMON FIELD KIT.
Flashlight, backup light, batteries, first-aid kit, notebook, camera, water, phone charger, gloves and enough common sense to go home.
RULE 05: FOLKLORE IS A LEAD, NOT PROOF.
Salt, iron, bells, prayers, symbols and local customs vary by tradition. Do not treat a forum post as a universal rule.
